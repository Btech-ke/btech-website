💡 Quick Tips

1
KUCCPS opens every May–June for university placement applications.
2
Minimum C+ required for most degree programs at public universities.
3
KMTC needs C− for certificate and C for diploma nursing programs.
4
Cluster points use 4 specific subjects per course — use the calculator above.
5
HELB loans available to all students at accredited institutions, apply online.
