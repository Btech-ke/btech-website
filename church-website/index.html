The Legacy of Mama Susana Nyabulwa

From deep sorrow came divine purpose. In 1963, after losing her daughter Hellena, Mama Susana received a heavenly visitation that would transform not only her life but countless others across Western Kenya and beyond.

Divine Calling & Prophetic Ministry

When an angel of God appeared to comfort Mama Susana in her grief, declaring that God had "prepared something bigger for her," it marked the beginning of a powerful ministry. The Spirit of God rested upon her, filling her with compassion for the poor and sick, and strength to keep the Sabbath holy.

What began as "Duond Wach Mokor" (The Voice of Prophecy) in a small fellowship has grown into God's Last Appeal Church - a movement that spans from Homa Bay to Nairobi, touching lives through healing, prayer, and unwavering faith in God's word.

Today, we continue Mama Susana's vision of a church that addresses both spiritual and practical needs, remaining inclusive while staying true to biblical principles and Sabbath observance.

Our Journey Through Time

From divine calling to transformative ministry

1959

The Beginning at Nyambare

Mama Susana and fellow believers established the Nyambare centre in Gem, Asumbi. In tents, they held powerful meetings, sharing the gospel and calling people to faith through the Spirit's leading.

1963

Official Church Foundation

After her expulsion from SDA church due to her prophetic revelations, Mama Susana officially founded "Duond Wach Mokor" (The Voice of Prophecy) church in South Nyanza, marking the birth of what would become God's Last Appeal Church.

1970s

Revival & Expansion

Great revival movements spread as testimonies of divine intervention, notably the healings of Mzee Zedekia Oduma and Mama Opiyo Matewa, drew attention to the ministry. The church expanded to Yimbo, Bondo, and other parts of South & Central Nyanza.

1990s

Structural Organization

The movement developed into a structured faith community with growing membership, organized into local congregations and regional centers with headquarters in Homa Bay County.

Present

Nationwide Ministry

Today, God's Last Appeal Church has branches in Awendo, Migori, Busia, Mombasa, and Nairobi, continuing to be a beacon of faith, healing, and inclusiveness while maintaining our core values of Sabbath observance and prophetic ministry.
