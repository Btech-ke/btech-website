    The Legacy of Mama Susana Nyabulwa

    From deep sorrow came divine purpose. In 1963, after losing her daughter Hellena, Mama Susana received a heavenly visitation that would transform not only her life but countless others across Western Kenya and beyond.

    Divine Calling & Prophetic Ministry

    When an angel of God appeared to comfort Mama Susana in her grief, declaring that God had "prepared something bigger for her," it marked the beginning of a powerful ministry. The Spirit of God rested upon her, filling her with compassion for the poor and sick, and strength to keep the Sabbath holy.

    What began as "Duond Wach Mokor" (The Voice of Prophecy) in a small fellowship has grown into God's Last Appeal Church - a movement that spans from Homa Bay to Nairobi, touching lives through healing, prayer, and unwavering faith in God's word.

    Today, we continue Mama Susana's vision of a church that addresses both spiritual and practical needs, remaining inclusive while staying true to biblical principles and Sabbath observance.
