Know your exact score

Enter your KCSE grades and see your cluster score out of 48, what you qualify for, and which universities and programmes are open to you.

How cluster points work Your cluster score = Maths + (English or Kiswahili, whichever is higher) + your 2 best other subjects. Each subject is scored out of 12 (A = 12, A− = 11 ... down to E = 1). Maximum possible = 48 points.
